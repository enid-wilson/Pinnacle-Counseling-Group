# Pinnacle Counseling Group — Proposed Copy Revisions

A page-by-page review of every visible piece of copy on the site (excluding provider bios, privacy/legal pages, and lightbox form labels, per scope). Each section shows:

- **CURRENT** — what's on the site right now
- **PROPOSED** — recommended revision
- **WHY** — the rationale (SEO/GEO, tone, or both)

For sections that are working as-is, you'll see **NO CHANGE — keep as is.**

**How to use:** edit directly inline in this file. Strike through proposals you reject. Rewrite proposals you want refined. Add notes. Return the marked-up doc and I'll implement only your accepted changes.

**Tone direction:** drop the explicit affluence signaling ("high-standard," "operate at," "any other professional relationship at this level") while keeping the luxury feel through restraint, precision, and warmth. Lean second-person ("you") over third-person ("our clients"). Add specialty signals (anxiety, OCD, eating disorders, attachment, modern relational) naturally where they help SEO/GEO.
