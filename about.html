About the Practice

Our origin.

Pinnacle Counseling Group offers a private, bespoke approach to clinical psychotherapy. Serving Sacramento and Gold River, our practice pairs advanced clinical expertise with a premium client experience.

Our Approach ✶

Beyond labels.

— I

Evidence-based, not formulaic

Our work is anchored in the latest evidence-based research, but we reject the limits of one-size-fits-all treatment. Your plan is built specifically for your life and goals.

— II

The whole person

We view all clients through a non-pathologizing lens — considering your history, your environment, and what you're working toward, not just the presenting concern or diagnosis. The work spans where you've been and where you're going.

— III

Concierge by design

From the feel of our offices to our clinical approach to how we handle intakes, every detail is built with warmth, privacy, and discretion in mind.

A Letter from the Founder

Why I built this practice.

I founded Pinnacle Counseling Group on a single conviction — that rigorous clinical work and genuine warmth belong together. My background bridges corporate strategy and integrative wellness, and over the years, I've noticed that true hospitality is missing from many mental health settings. I built this practice so you never have to choose between clinical excellence and an intentional client experience.

As President and Clinical Supervisor, my focus is on protecting our clinical standards and mentoring the associate clinicians joining our practice, while continuing to personally see a small, dedicated caseload of individuals and couples. My work is anchored in psychodynamic theory and an unwavering respect for the whole person — your history, your environment, and exactly what you are working toward.

If any of this resonates, I'd welcome the chance to connect with you.

"Therapy should feel as intentional as any other meaningful investment in your life."

— Enid Wilson, LMFT Founder · President · Clinical Supervisor

Our Values ✶

What we stand on.

Four commitments that shape every decision we make — from clinical practice to the experience of walking through our door.

— I

Exceptional Care

A commitment to clinical integrity and a non-pathologizing approach — care that holds space for your history, your environment, and your aspirations.

— II

Intentional Hospitality

A seamless, tailored experience — from the design of our physical spaces to the process before your first session — designed to honor your time, privacy, and the significance of your investment in yourself.

— III

Enduring Impact

We focus on outcomes that support the long-term resilience and legacy of the individuals and families we serve — not stopping at symptom management.

— IV

Community Stewardship

We're invested in the greater Sacramento region — and in the families and institutions that shape it.

Community

Enid Wilson, LMFT

President  ·  California LMFT #149114 Session rates  ·  $200 individual  ·  $225 couples

With a background in both corporate strategy and integrative wellness, Enid brings a grounded and versatile perspective to her role as President of Pinnacle Counseling Group. Her approach is rooted in a commitment to clinical integrity and a deep respect for the whole person, dedicated to fostering the emotional health of our local communities.

As a therapist, I am passionate about helping individuals and couples to heal from a harmful event or relationship, grow their sense of self and satisfaction, and gain insight into themselves and their relationships.

Clinical Approach

I leverage the following modalities, always customizing treatment to the unique situation and needs of each individual:

  • Psychodynamic
  • Internal Family Systems (IFS)
  • Emotion Focused Therapy (EFT)
  • DBT and Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)
  • Trauma-informed somatic

Education

  • Master of Arts, Marriage and Family Therapy — Touro University
  • Psychology Graduate Studies — Drexel University
  • Bachelor of Arts, English Literature & Communications Studies — University of Portland
  • Certified in Dialectical Behavior Therapy (C-DBT)
  • Certified Clinical Trauma Professional (CCTP)

Community Impact

I am passionate about community and giving back. I currently sit on the Alumnae Board at Saint Francis High School here in Sacramento and am a past secretary of the Sacramento Valley chapter of the California Association of Marriage and Family Therapists.

Previous volunteer experience includes Dorchester County Mobile Crisis Unit, Wounded Warrior Project, US Air Force Spouses wellness, and the National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI).

Corporate Consulting

In addition to my private practice, I work in corporate communications and digital strategy and have served as a coach for managers and executives. I draw on my experience in large, publicly-traded companies to support clients with issues related to their careers and work stress.
