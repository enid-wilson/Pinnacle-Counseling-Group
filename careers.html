Associate Marriage &

At Pinnacle Counseling Group, we provide a seamless, professional experience for our clients and a supportive, growth-oriented environment for our clinicians. We are dedicated to the long-term success of our Associates, providing the tools and mentorship necessary to build a thriving caseload and prepare you for a successful future in private practice, if you choose.

Compensation & Incentive Structure

Transparent & Competitive Pay

$50/hr
Clinical Rate

For all billable client sessions. Your clinical time is fully compensated at a competitive rate aligned with the Sacramento market.

$20/hr
Non-Clinical Rate

For documentation, required supervision, approved meetings, and administrative tasks.

+$15

Performance Bonus Structure

Employees are eligible for bonuses tied to caseload volume. The 2026 structure includes an additional $10/hr for clinical hours between 21–24, and an additional $15/hr for all clinical hours over 24.

The Environment

Clinical Excellence in a
Healthy Environment.

Presence
To ensure a focused healing environment, computers and phones are kept out of sessions, except for scheduling purposes. Your full presence is expected — and protected.
Documentation
We utilize SimplePractice for EHR. The NoteTaker AI tool is available only with explicit, written client consent.
Dress Code
We maintain a professional appearance that reflects our brand — closed-toe shoes required; blue jeans are not permitted during working hours.
Professionalism
Clinicians are expected to act ethically and professionally, and to be fully dedicated to the care of existing and future clients.
Client Options
Sessions are offered in-person at our Gold River office and via telehealth. We serve adult individuals, couples, and minors.

Pinnacle was founded on the belief that rigorous clinical excellence should coexist with intentional hospitality.

Our Culture & Values

Pinnacle Counseling Group is built on four core values. We are looking for clinicians who are eager to grow their skills within a structured, high-standard professional environment.

Exceptional Care

A commitment to rigorous clinical integrity and a non-pathologizing, whole-person approach to healing.

Intentional Hospitality

High professionalism. High care. A seamless experience that allows both clinicians and clients to reach their highest potential.

Enduring Impact

Focusing on transformative outcomes that support the long-term resilience and legacy of the families we serve.

Community Stewardship

Serving as a reliable professional pillar dedicated to the collective vitality of the Greater Sacramento region.
