Our Services

Specialized care, at every stage.

Psychotherapy and consultation for individuals, couples, families, and the organizations that shape our community.

Individual Therapy

Individual psychotherapy for adults navigating relationship challenges, high-functioning anxiety, OCD, eating disorders, trauma, and significant life transitions. Deep, relational work is paired with evidence-based tools for lasting clarity.

Couples Counseling

Couples counseling rooted in modern relational and attachment-based therapy, including Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) and Gottman frameworks. Designed for couples navigating transitions, deeper connection, or healing from rifts.

Children & Teens

Specialized support for young people navigating identity, academic pressure, social challenges, family change, and the complexities of growing up.

Organizational Consulting

Strategic support for leaders and organizations seeking to improve workplace culture, team dynamics, and organizational wellbeing.

Clinical Supervision

Individual and group supervision for California AMFTs and APCCs, grounded in rigorous clinical standards, a psychodynamic approach, and a commitment to your growth in the field.

Enid Wilson, LMFT

President  ·  California LMFT #149114 Session rates  ·  $200 individual  ·  $225 couples

With a background in both corporate strategy and integrative wellness, Enid brings a grounded and versatile perspective to her role as President of Pinnacle Counseling Group. Her approach is rooted in a commitment to clinical integrity and a deep respect for the whole person, dedicated to fostering the emotional health of our local communities.

As a therapist, I am passionate about helping individuals and couples to heal from a harmful event or relationship, grow their sense of self and satisfaction, and gain insight into themselves and their relationships.

Clinical Approach

I leverage the following modalities, always customizing treatment to the unique situation and needs of each individual:

  • Psychodynamic
  • Internal Family Systems (IFS)
  • Emotion Focused Therapy (EFT)
  • DBT and Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)
  • Trauma-informed somatic

Education

  • Master of Arts, Marriage and Family Therapy — Touro University
  • Psychology Graduate Studies — Drexel University
  • Bachelor of Arts, English Literature & Communications Studies — University of Portland
  • Certified in Dialectical Behavior Therapy (C-DBT)
  • Certified Clinical Trauma Professional (CCTP)

Community Impact

I am passionate about community and giving back. I currently sit on the Alumnae Board at Saint Francis High School here in Sacramento and am a past secretary of the Sacramento Valley chapter of the California Association of Marriage and Family Therapists.

Previous volunteer experience includes Dorchester County Mobile Crisis Unit, Wounded Warrior Project, US Air Force Spouses wellness, and the National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI).

Corporate Consulting

In addition to my private practice, I work in corporate communications and digital strategy and have served as a coach for managers and executives. I draw on my experience in large, publicly-traded companies to support clients with issues related to their careers and work stress.

Giana Azizeh, LMFT

Advisor  ·  California LMFT #157294

Giana Azizeh, LMFT #157294, is a relational therapist experienced in working with couples, older adults, and individuals navigating trauma, anxiety disorders, and infidelity. Her clinical approach is rooted in Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) and integrates attachment theory, psychodynamic therapy, and Internal Family Systems (IFS).

She earned her Master's degree in Marital and Family Therapy from Alliant International University in Sacramento and her Bachelor's degree in Psychology with a minor in Gerontology from the Sanford Center for Aging at the University of Nevada, Reno.

During her undergraduate studies, Giana worked as a research assistant contributing to published research focused on anticipatory grief among terminally ill patients. Her background also includes volunteer work with older adults through intergenerational support programs, as well as non-clinical professional experience supporting foster youth and adults experiencing severe mental illness in community-based settings.

Giana has been in private practice in Sacramento, California since 2025. She completed the Emotionally Focused Therapy Externship through ICEEFT and is a member of the California Association of Marriage and Family Therapists (CAMFT).

Evangeline Wilson, AMFT

Associate Therapist  ·  California AMFT #163690 Supervised by Enid Wilson, LMFT #149114 Session rate  ·  $160

Every path to parenthood is unique. As a bereaved parent, I bring both professional expertise and personal understanding to work with clients navigating infertility, pregnancy loss, grief, and parenting after loss. I’m especially passionate about walking alongside individuals and couples through miscarriage and the unexpected emotional landscape of postpartum adjustment.

Whether you’re feeling hopeless, disconnected, anxious, or overwhelmed, together we can explore the emotions, decisions, and questions that arise along the way. My clinical approach is grounded in Solution-Focused Brief Therapy and attachment theory, delivered in a trauma-informed frame. I offer a collaborative, safe space where we can begin to process grief and recognize the resilience already within you.

Whatever your journey to building your family looks like, you don’t have to navigate it alone. Together, we can create space for healing, hope, and moving forward in a way that feels right for you.

Cheryl Runkle, AMFT, APCC

Associate Therapist  ·  California AMFT #161250  ·  APCC #21829 Supervised by Enid Wilson, LMFT #149114 Session rate  ·  $160

Cheryl believes therapy is most effective when it is grounded in genuine connection, curiosity, and deep respect for each person’s lived experience. Her approach is person-centered and relational, with a focus on understanding your unique story, strengths, and values. She strives to create a supportive, collaborative space where you feel truly heard, understood, and empowered to show up exactly as you are.

Cheryl’s therapeutic style is compassionate, thoughtful, and grounded in trauma-informed care. She integrates cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and strengths-based approaches to help clients better understand their thoughts, emotions, and patterns while building on the skills and resilience they already possess. She has experience supporting clients through trauma, life stressors, and challenging transitions, and she is trained in Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR).

She has a particular interest in working with caregivers and individuals navigating major life transitions. As the surviving spouse of a disabled Navy veteran, she feels especially connected to and passionate about supporting military spouses and others within the military community.

She is committed to walking alongside clients as they move toward meaningful growth, healing, and lasting change.
