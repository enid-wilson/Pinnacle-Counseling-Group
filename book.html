What to Expect

Your first step.

01

Choose your therapist

Read our provider bios and pick the therapist whose focus areas and approach resonate with what you're working on. Not sure? Call or text (916) 990-2326 or email Info@PinnacleCounselingGroup.com — our clinical director is happy to help you find the right fit.

02

Book your consultation

Schedule a free 15-minute call directly with the therapist you selected. No cost, no commitment — a brief, confidential conversation to see if it's a good match.

03

Move forward together

On the call, share your goals, ask questions, and — if it feels right — schedule your first session. We handle the paperwork and logistics from there.

Good to Know

Practical details.

Session Rates

Licensed clinicians $200–225. Associate clinicians $160. Sessions are fifty minutes unless you'd like to coordinate additional time. Sliding scale available. Please inquire.

Insurance

PPO plans accepted include Aetna and Optum. Superbills provided for out-of-network reimbursement (licensed therapists only). We're happy to help you check coverage on the call.

Telehealth

Available throughout California. In-person sessions at our Sacramento and Gold River offices.

Response Time

Consultation calls are typically scheduled within one to two business days.
